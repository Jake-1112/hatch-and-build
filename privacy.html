Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This policy describes what information hatchandbuild.com (“Hatch & Build”, “we”, “us”) collects and how it is used. The short version: this site has no user accounts, asks for no personal details, and stores almost nothing — with two exceptions explained below (the public Live Wall, and advertising cookies).

What this policy covers

This policy applies to hatchandbuild.com and its blog pages. The individual apps linked from this site — Drawing Playground, GuGu, DANA, and Hangeulmaru — are separate services with their own data practices; please refer to the information provided within each app or site.

The Live Wall

The home page includes a “Live Wall” where visitors can post a short message, a drawing, or a photo. Please understand how it works before posting:

  • Everything you post is public, immediately. Posts appear in real time to every visitor of this site.
  • Posts are stored on our server until the wall is reset, at which point they are deleted.
  • We do not collect your name, email, or any account information when you post. Posts are not linked to your identity by us.
  • Do not post personal information (yours or anyone else's), and do not post photos of people without their permission.
  • We may remove any post at our discretion. To request removal of a post, contact us and we will take it down.

Information we do not collect

This site has no sign-up, no login for visitors, no newsletter, and no forms that ask for personal details. We do not sell any information. Our own server does not set tracking cookies; standard technical data (such as your IP address) is processed only as necessary to deliver the site to your browser.

Local storage

The site uses your browser's local storage for one thing: keeping the site owner's admin session token after the owner logs in. Regular visitors never log in, and nothing about your visit is saved to your browser by us.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads on this site. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.

For more detail on how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Children

This site is a general-audience website and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has posted personal information on the Live Wall, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change (for example, if we add analytics or new features that handle data), we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a removal request? Email hatchandbuild@gmail.com or use the contact page.
